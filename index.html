4getMeNot is your electronic agenda for securely storing email and web addresses, phone numbers, and text data—from postal addresses to personal notes. It dials phones, launches your email client or browser, imports from Address Book, Eudora, vCards, LDIF/CSV, and exports to vCard or LDIF/CSV for seamless compatibility.

Smart Import & Export

Print & Remind
